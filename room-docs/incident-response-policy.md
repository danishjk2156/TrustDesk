# Incident response policy (AcmeCloud)

Owner: Security Lead · Legal review: Legal · Reviewed: 2026-09-01

1. All administrative API activity is recorded in a multi-region audit trail with log file
   integrity validation. Application and network logs are kept for 365 days.
2. Security incidents are triaged by the on-call engineer within 1 hour.
3. **Breach notification: affected customers are notified within 72 hours of AcmeCloud
   confirming a personal-data breach.** Any shorter promise needs Legal approval.
4. Vulnerabilities can be reported to security@acmecloud.example; reports are acknowledged
   within 2 business days.
5. The database keeps 35 days of automated backups across multiple availability zones.
